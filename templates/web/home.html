Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to help you navigate and use WAJ VTU confidently.

What is WAJ VTU? ▼
WAJ VTU is a digital service platform that allows Nigerian users to conveniently buy data bundles, airtime recharges, pay electricity bills, renew cable TV decoders, and buy exam registration PINs from one synchronized account.
What services can I use on WAJ VTU? ▼
You can purchase Data Bundles (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile), Airtime Recharges, Cable TV Subscriptions (DSTV, GOtv, StarTimes), Electricity Bill Payments (prepaid meter tokens and postpaid bills), and Exam Registration PINs (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB).
How do I create an account? ▼
Click "Create Account" or "Get Started", provide your full name, valid email address, phone number, and a secure password. Your account will be activated immediately without any setup fees.
How do I fund my wallet? ▼
Log into your dashboard, click "Fund Wallet", enter the amount you wish to add, and complete the payment securely using Paystack with your debit card or bank transfer. Your wallet is credited instantly upon payment confirmation.
How do I buy data? ▼
From your dashboard, click "Data Bundles", choose your network (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile), select your preferred plan size and duration, input the recipient phone number, and confirm. Your data is delivered immediately to the recipient line.
How do I buy airtime? ▼
Select "Airtime Recharge", pick the network provider, enter the recipient phone number and amount, then click purchase. The airtime is credited directly to the phone line within seconds.
Can I view my transaction history? ▼
Yes. Navigate to "Transactions" in the top menu to view an exhaustive record of all purchases, wallet deposits, reference numbers, date stamps, and transaction statuses.
How does the referral program work? ▼
Once registered, copy your unique referral link from your referral page. Share it with friends. When a friend signs up through your link and completes an eligible first purchase of ₦{{ "%.2f"|format(referral_min_tx) }} or more, an automatic referral reward of ₦{{ "%.2f"|format(referral_reward) }} is instantly added to your wallet balance.
What happens if my transaction fails? ▼
If an automated delivery fails due to temporary network downtime at the telecom provider, your wallet is not debited (or is promptly returned). You can check the transaction status in your history or contact support via WhatsApp with your reference ID for immediate investigation.
How can I contact support? ▼
You can reach our customer support directly on WhatsApp at {{ support_phone }}, call our customer service line, or email us. We are available to help resolve any inquiry promptly.
Can I use WAJ VTU on my phone? ▼
Yes! WAJ VTU is 100% responsive and optimized for mobile browsers. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) onto your home screen, or access services directly through our WhatsApp bot!
