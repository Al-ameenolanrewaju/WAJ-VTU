Frequently Resolved Inquiries

Why can't I fill a support form directly on this website?

To prevent unauthorized ticket tampering, impersonation, and delayed carrier lookups, all official dispute forms are filled exclusively inside our WhatsApp chatbot. This immediately connects your WhatsApp phone identity with the telecom gateway transaction logs.

What happens if my data bundle hasn't delivered after payment?

Telecom networks process data top-ups in 5 to 60 seconds. If an operator suffers congestion, the transaction enters PENDING. If the operator fails the delivery, your wallet is automatically returned. You can also paste your reference code above to check the latest provider response.

How do I request an official receipt sent to my email?

You can download receipts directly from your Transactions page by clicking on any transaction, or message our WhatsApp bot with "send receipt for my last transaction".

How can I speak to a live human agent?

You can request human assistance or fill an official dispute form directly inside our WhatsApp chatbot by sending "speak to agent" or "support form". Our system immediately alerts our human supervisor desk to respond directly in your chat.
